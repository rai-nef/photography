The Unseen World

Right beneath our feet lies a universe most of us never stop to see.

The world has always been full of wonders — we just need the right lens to find them. The Unseen World is a macro photography series that ventures deep into the miniature wilderness that surrounds us every day. Through extreme close-ups, insects reveal themselves as extraordinary beings: compound eyes that hold entire galaxies within them, wings that rival stained glass in their intricacy, and behaviours that speak of instinct, survival, and quiet beauty. This series is an invitation to pause, to look again, and to find that the smallest things in the world often carry the greatest stories.
