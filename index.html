My Photography

MONOCHROME | MULTIPLE GENRES

Magical Monochromes

When colour steps aside, truth steps forward. Magical Monochromes is a curated collection of black-and-white photographs spanning multiple genres — from the grit of street photography to the stillness of portraiture and beyond. Stripped of colour, each frame speaks through light, texture, and shadow alone. These are not colourless images; they are images that reveal the full depth of a moment, undistracted and unapologetic.

TRAVEL | ACROSS INDIA

Colours of India

India does not whisper — it sings in a thousand colours. Colours of India is a travel photography series gathered from the length and breadth of this extraordinary land. From the saffron-dusted ghats of Varanasi to the riot of festivities in Bengal, from golden deserts to emerald valleys — every frame is a celebration. This is India seen through my lens: vivid, alive, and endlessly layered with culture, tradition, and heritage.

View case study
MACRO | THE TINY UNIVERSE

The Unseen World

Right beneath our feet, hidden in blades of grass and curled leaves, exists a world most of us never stop to see. The Unseen World is a macro photography series that ventures deep into the miniature wilderness of insects and tiny creatures. Up close, the ordinary becomes extraordinary — a dewdrop becomes an ocean, a leaf becomes a landscape, and a tiny insect becomes the quiet hero of its own epic story. Step in, and discover the universe hiding in plain sight.

About

A Visual Storyteller
rooted in nature.

Gaurav Rai is a photographer and graphic designer from West Bengal, India — a visual storyteller whose eye for the extraordinary finds its deepest roots in the natural world. Over eight prolific years behind the lens, he has carved a distinctive voice across multiple photographic genres, with a particular mastery in macro and monochrome photography.

His work has earned international recognition, including Gold and Silver Medals in prestigious PSA circuits, alongside wide acceptance across international salons. His photographs have graced exhibitions in Dubai, China, and India — compelling visual narratives that resonate with audiences far beyond borders. His artistry has been celebrated in renowned publications, with a notable feature in OTHELO, India's pioneering Black and White Photography Magazine. Most recently, he was honoured as the winner of the HIPAE World Camera Day Photography Contest in Dubai.

Beyond the camera, Gaurav brings over four years of experience in graphic design — spanning education, healthcare, hospitality, and entertainment. Nature, in his eyes, is not merely a subject but the ultimate teacher, shaping both his aesthetics and his philosophy. Tech-savvy and conceptually driven, he approaches every project with the conviction that design has the power to spark genuine change.
