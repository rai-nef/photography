Colours of India

India does not whisper — it sings in a thousand colours.

India is not just a country; it is a feeling — vast, layered, and endlessly alive. Colours of India is a travel photography series gathered from journeys across the length and breadth of this extraordinary land. From the saffron-dusted ghats of Varanasi to the riot of festivity in Bengal, from the quiet symmetry of ancient temples to the exuberant burst of folk celebrations — every photograph in this collection is a window into a world that is at once ancient and ever-renewing. Through these frames, I invite you to experience the vivid richness of India's culture, tradition, and heritage — a heritage that belongs not just to its people, but to the whole world.
